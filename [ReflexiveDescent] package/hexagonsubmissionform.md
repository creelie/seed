Title: Reflexive Descent to Minimal Seeds: Uniform Bounds and Free Sums

Abstract:
We study saturated descents through reflexive lattice subpolytopes to r-minimal seeds. The uniform-existence conjecture has an affirmative answer: every descent terminates, and a published volume bound gives the classification-free estimate $D_d\le2(s_d-1)^2-2$ for $d\ge4$. We give an exact finite construction of a descent and prove that the reflexive-subpolytope order of a free sum in a direct-sum lattice is the product of the factor orders. Shortest and longest descent lengths are therefore additive. An explicit family in dimension $2r$ has shortest length $r$ and longest length $2r$, proving that both depth and the gap between these lengths can grow with dimension. A second base, checked by two exact integer enumerators, gives lengths $5r$ and $6r$ and a complete formula for the descent counts. The extremal depths are superadditive, and $D_d/d$ has an extended limit. In dimension four the earlier bound of $348$ for every descent remains valid; twelve interval certificates refine the shortest-depth range to $22\le D_4\le346$ relative to the supplied Kreuzer--Skarke census. The exact value of $D_4$ and the optimal growth rate are not determined.
