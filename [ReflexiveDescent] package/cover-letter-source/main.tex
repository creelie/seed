\begin{letter}{Editors\\Discrete \& Computational Geometry}
\opening{Dear Editors,}
Please consider \emph{Reflexive Descent to Minimal Seeds: Uniform Bounds and
Free Sums} as a research article. This is a cover-letter draft for the
author's review before an actual submission.

The manuscript studies saturated inclusions of reflexive lattice polytopes.
It proves that the reflexive-subpolytope order of a free sum in a direct-sum
lattice is the product of the factor orders, giving exact addition of
shortest and longest descent lengths and explicit families of increasing
depth. The topic concerns lattice polytopes, convex hulls and geometric
inclusion orders.

An earlier joint manuscript with Ushashi Bhattacharya is posted at
\url{https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00456v1}. Her contribution is credited in
the title footnote and bibliography. The present revision adds the free-sum
results, an independently checked planar base, a census-relative interval
refinement and a scoped Lean supplement. The exact four-dimensional maximum
remains undetermined; the whole manuscript is not formally verified.

The accompanying release contains raw TeX, data, verification scripts and
Lean sources with the exact coverage and limitations. It records the
assistance used to prepare the proofs and text. Funding and competing
interest statements are included in the manuscript.

The author will complete the journal's required declarations in the
submission portal after reviewing the final files.
